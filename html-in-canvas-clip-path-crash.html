HTML-in-canvas: animated clip-path crashes the tab

Needs Chrome Canary with chrome://flags/#canvas-draw-element enabled. A WebGL2 <canvas layoutsubtree> re-uploads its child with texElementSubImage2D on every paint event. Inside the child, images reveal on link hover through a clip-path: inset() transition.

Steps: keep "Animate clip-path" checked and hover the links a few times. The tab crashes. Uncheck it and hover again: the same clip-path change without a transition works fine.
